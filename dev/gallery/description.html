A page for journal managers that shows how the active submissions (neither published nor declined) are distributed among the editors, under the new left menu entry Editor assignments:

  • one row per editor (all section editors, and everybody else assigned as editor) with the number of submissions and a table of them: ID, authors and title, linked to the workflow;
  • Email editor: a prefilled message about the submission, recorded in its email log;
  • Transfer to other editor: assigns another journal manager or section editor and removes the previous one, optionally with OJS's email "Editor assigned";
  • Remove assignment, and Assign editor for submissions without editor;
  • changes are recorded in the submission's activity log, as with OJS's participants list;
  • English and German.
